Tab Summaries

Pick a date range and run an automated, numbers-grounded summary of a dashboard tab. Defaults to yesterday vs the day prior.

Single-day range (Start = End) compares that day to the day before. A multi-day range compares to the equal-length window immediately before it.

Analysis instructions & context

Guidance for the AI summary — what to focus on, known launches/tests, how to interpret moves. Saved server-side and applied on every device. It steers focus only; it can't add numbers the data doesn't contain.
