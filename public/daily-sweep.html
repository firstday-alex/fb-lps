Daily Sweep

Runs every report for one day against the day before, and keeps the moves that are bigger than normal day-to-day noise

Compares the day with the day prior, and with the same weekday last week to separate real moves from weekday pattern.

Reports checked

Each check calls the report's own data, so a flagged number is the one the tab shows.

    How a move gets flagged. Counts and rates are tested against What Changed's measured day-to-day noise (Poisson or binomial plus the extra variance the store's history shows), and every list is checked against its own spread so a noisy level like ads can't flood the results. AOV uses each day's order-level spread. Session duration, CPM, CTR, spend and discount depth have no usable variance, so they use a size bar instead (shown as "threshold"). Flags worth under $250 are dropped.
    Unusual = beyond noise against the day prior and against the same weekday last week, in the same direction. Weekday pattern = beyond noise against the day prior only — most often just the normal weekday shape. Dollar figures are estimates of one move each and overlap across reports, so don't add them up.
