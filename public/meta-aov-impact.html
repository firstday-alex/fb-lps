Meta Paid Social AOV Impact

Loads Shopify orders for a date range and the previous period, keeps only orders whose first visit carries the utm_source / utm_medium below, and decomposes Δ Revenue + Δ Blended AOV across campaigns, ads and landing pages.

Revenue Decomposition by campaign

Each utm_campaign's contribution to Δ Revenue inside this Meta segment, split into: Orders Effect = ΔOrders × prevAOV (more/fewer orders at the old basket), AOV Effect = prevOrders × ΔAOV (same orders earning a higher/lower basket), and Interaction = ΔOrders × ΔAOV (the cross-term). The three sum to Δ Revenue for that campaign; rows sum to the segment's Δ Revenue.

Why is AOV moving?  (units/order × price/unit, by campaign)

AOV is identical to UPT × ASP: units per transaction (basket size) times average selling price per unit. UPT Effect = ΔUPT × prevASP (basket-size move at the old price). ASP Effect = prevUPT × ΔASP (per-unit price move at the old basket). Interaction = ΔUPT × ΔASP. The three sum to Δ AOV for that campaign. If UPT Effect dominates → people are buying fewer items per order. If ASP Effect dominates → average unit price fell (discounts or mix-shift to cheaper SKUs).

Blended AOV Decomposition by campaign

Decomposes the change in this segment's blended AOV (Meta revenue ÷ Meta orders) into per-campaign contributions. Own-AOV Effect = prevOrderShare × ΔAOV (the campaign's own AOV move, weighted by its share of last period's Meta orders). Mix-Shift Effect = ΔOrderShare × (currAOV − blended avg) (gaining/losing orders at a basket above or below the blended average). The two sum to the campaign's contribution; rows sum to total Δ Blended AOV.

Attribution is read from each order's customerJourneySummary.firstVisit — an order counts toward this segment when that first visit carries the utm_source/utm_medium you loaded with. Orders with no recorded first visit, or a different source/medium, are excluded entirely (they are not bucketed as Unknown here). Campaign / ad / landing page all come from that same first visit, so a blank value shows as (none). AOV = total_price ÷ orders; UPT = units ÷ orders; ASP = total_price ÷ units. Revenue is order total_price (incl. tax/shipping, currency = shop currency); units is subtotalLineItemsQuantity. This is order-level first-touch attribution, not Shopify session data — so order counts here will not tie exactly to the session-based transactions on the Meta CVR Impact tab.
