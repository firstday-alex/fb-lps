Why is gross AOV moving?  (units/order × price/unit)

Gross AOV (order total incl. shipping, tax & protection) is identical to UPT × ASP, where UPT counts every line — protection and free gifts included. For product-only figures see the Net AOV section above. Here,: units per transaction (basket size) times average selling price per unit. UPT Effect = ΔUPT × prevASP (basket-size move at the old price). ASP Effect = prevUPT × ΔASP (per-unit price move at the old basket). Interaction = ΔUPT × ΔASP. The three sum to Δ gross AOV for that source. If UPT Effect dominates → people are buying fewer items per order. If ASP Effect dominates → average unit price fell (discounts or mix-shift to cheaper SKUs).

Blended Gross AOV Decomposition

Decomposes the change in blended gross AOV (overall order revenue ÷ overall orders) into per-utm_source contributions. Own Gross-AOV Effect = prevOrderShare × ΔAOV (this source's own gross-AOV move, weighted by its share of last period's orders). Mix-Shift Effect = ΔOrderShare × (currAOV − blended avg) (gaining/losing orders at a basket above or below the blended average). The two sum to this source's contribution; rows sum to total Δ Blended Gross AOV.

Meta net AOV: own move vs mix shift

Net AOV, as in the Net AOV section at the top: paid product revenue after every discount ÷ orders, so shipping, tax and protection are out and free gifts or $0 lines don't count as units. It follows that section's "$0 orders" toggle. Δ Meta net AOV splits into each bid strategy's own move (its net AOV changed, at its old share of orders) and mix shift (its share of orders changed). The own move is split again into units per order, list price per unit and discount depth. A group with no orders in one period counts entirely as mix.

Facebook ad AOV outliers

Finds individual ads (utm_content) whose gross AOV is far above or below the Meta average for the same window, for orders whose first visit had utm_source facebook or Facebook. An ad counts as an outlier when it has at least the minimum orders and its AOV gap is at least 2 standard errors from the Meta AOV, then Claude writes up what stands out. Pulls orders again, so a 7-day range takes about half a minute before the write-up starts.

Attribution is read from each order's customerJourneySummary.firstVisit. Orders without a recorded first visit are bucketed as Unknown. Gross AOV = order total (incl. shipping, tax & protection) ÷ orders. Revenue is order total_price (incl. tax/shipping, currency = shop currency).
